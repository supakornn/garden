Book by **Eric Evans**

### Chapter 1 Crunching Knowledge

- The hard part is figuring out how the business really works.
- Developers learn from domain experts who understand the day to day work and rules.
- This process of learning together is called **Knowledge Crunching**.
- The team uses what it learns to build a **Domain Model**.
- A good **deep model** explains the important business ideas, not only screens or database tables.

### Chapter 2 Communication and the Use of Language

- **Ubiquitous Language** means the whole team uses the same words for the same business ideas.
- Use those words in conversations, code, tests, and documents.
- Talk through real examples when a word or rule is unclear.
- Update the model and code when the team finds a better term or understanding.
- The software should use the same language as the business.
